How to personalize your Christmas pack?

Start by choosing your quantity, from 1 to 100 images. The package of €22.90 remains the same on this beach. Then select the format suited to the intended use: a portrait gives space to a silhouette, while a landscape leaves more space for the environment.

If your project relies on a reference face, choose a model that accepts it. A sharp, well-lit image makes the subject easier to read. A LoRA constitutes another method of customization, with its own compatibility constraints; it does not automatically replace a reference photo.

Questions about the Christmas pack.

What is the Christmas pack like?

Warm lights, decorative details and soft materials create an end-of-year holiday collection. The collection offers creative direction, not reporting or documentary reproduction. Decor elements are interpreted by the generation model.

How can I customise my Christmas images?

Choose the quantity, format and model in the studio. A reference can be added with a compatible model. A universe for cards, family creations and personalized seasonal content. The resemblance of a face and the detail of a location may vary depending on the model.

How much does the Christmas collection cost?

The package is €22.90 for an order of 1 to 100 images. The price remains the same if you choose less than 100 images. The amount and options should be checked before paying.

Are the four overviews contractual?

Published previews are validated builds for this collection. They illustrate a visual direction, without guaranteeing an identical result. If they are not yet validated, the page indicates this and does not replace the results with images from another pack.

What is included in the download?

The download packages the completed images into a ZIP. Internal instructions and scenarios are not delivered. Find the progress and the files available in your command space.

What time frame should you expect?

The two-hour objective remains subject to technical validation. The delivery time depends on the model, the volume requested, its availability and any possible returns. No guaranteed duration is announced before these checks.
