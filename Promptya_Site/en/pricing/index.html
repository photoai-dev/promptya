How does the package discount work?

Add one to four packs in the same basket. The price of each pack adjusts to the level reached: €22.90, €19.90, €16.90 or €14.90. The total amount is recalculated before payment. Purchases made in separate orders do not stack to achieve this tier.

You can choose several themes or add the same theme several times. Each line represents a series of 100 photos maximum. References, formats and models must be compatible with your project. The two-hour objective remains subject to technical validation.

The cover generator has its own page and its own 1:1 square format. Its price will be defined separately; it is not included in the above packages.
