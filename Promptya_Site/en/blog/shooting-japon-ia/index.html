Imaginary journeys

AI photo shoot in Japan: choose an atmosphere rather than clichés

A project inspired by Japan benefits from specifying its register: contemporary city, architecture, night light or calmer scene. This decision avoids bringing together signs that do not tell the same universe.

Distinguish between destination and city

A pack dedicated to Japan and a collection centered on Tokyo should not be read as two identical offers. The first can evoke several registers; the second specifies an urban intention. Compare the scenes described on the sheets in order to choose the family of decors that corresponds to your project.

Match the color to the garment

A colorful night scene can strongly modify the perception of skin tone and materials. A simple outfit provides a point of reference in a dense setting. If fidelity to the face is essential, also choose portraits in a less busy atmosphere to facilitate comparison with the reference.

Avoid decorative accessories without a role

Each accessory must help the reading of the character or scene. An accumulation of symbols can produce a generic image. Prioritize framing and atmosphere, then check decor text and architectural lines before selecting results.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

For a graphic series, choose a short night palette and a plain garment. The illustration shows colored light work, without representing Japan. The Japan and Tokyo sheets allow you to compare the decors of these collections.

Before choosing your images

  • Choose an urban or calmer scene.
  • Maintain a readable face in colored lights.
  • Evaluate the details of the decor without trying to show everything.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
