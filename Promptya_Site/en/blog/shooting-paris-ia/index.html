Imaginary journeys

AI photo shoot in Paris: five atmospheres to compose your series

Paris offers several visual writings: stone architecture, monumental perspective, geometric glass roof or end of day light. A series benefits from choosing a common thread instead of piling up famous settings.

Choosing an architectural family

The Palais-Royal evokes arcades and repeated lines. The Louvre allows you to play with the diagonals of its pyramid. The Opéra Garnier suggests a more theatrical register. These markers don't tell the same story: choose the one that supports your outfit and your portrait intention.

Give a role to light

An atmosphere of low sun around the Eiffel Tower can create a different softness from a more graphic scene in front of the Louvre. Harmonize the outfit and values ​​of the face with this light. It is not enough to add a monument to create a coherent Parisian atmosphere.

Avoid the repetitive souvenir portrait

Alternate a silhouette integrated into the architecture, a closer portrait and an image where the decor becomes a background line. AI images are staged: present them as creations when the context could suggest a session actually photographed on site.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

For a sober series, start from the Louvre and the Palais-Royal: cream tones, legible architectural lines and an alternation of medium shots and silhouettes. The Paris pack sheet details the atmospheres offered and the previews available.

Before choosing your images

  • A main location to open the series.
  • Framings that change the relationship to the setting.
  • An outfit compatible with the stone palette.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
