Imaginary journeys

Mykonos or Santorini: which world to choose for your AI photos?

The two destinations share a palette of whites and blues, but they do not offer the same visual grammar. The choice depends on the place you want to give to the village, the horizon and the silhouette.

Promptya Editorial · October 16, 2026 · 2 min read

Mykonos for village paths

The alleys of Chora allow us to imagine a series based on movement and close perspectives. The mills and the waterfront bring wider breaths. This direction is suitable for an alternation of street scenes and portraits, with a simple or summery clothing register.

Santorini for volumes and openness

The domes and stepped facades give a more sculptural decor. The character can be thought of as a silhouette in this set of volumes. A long dress or a contemplative composition easily finds its place, provided you keep distinct contours between the subject and the background.

Choose a duo for two complementary stories

You can combine the two collections if each series has a different role. Keep a common palette, then reserve the alleys for one and the large open compositions for the other. Compare the sheets and overviews rather than considering the two worlds as interchangeable.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

For a duo, build a first walking sequence in Mykonos and a second, more relaxed one in Santorini. A common light outfit connects the two atmospheres without erasing their architectural differences.

Before choosing your images

  • Prefer alleys or open views.
  • Choose the place of the outfit in the story.
  • Define a different role if you take both.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
