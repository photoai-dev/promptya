Characters

Create a memorable AI mascot character: silhouette, attitude and story

A mascot becomes recognizable thanks to a few strong characteristics. A silhouette, a palette and an attitude can be enough to establish a character, provided that it remains consistent in its different representations.

Promptya Editorial · October 16, 2026 · 2 min read

Start with a recognizable silhouette

A hairstyle, build or clothing can serve as a distinctive sign. The character must remain recognizable in a wide shot as in a portrait. Avoid adding too many competing features: a clear identity is more easily remembered than an inventory of effects.

Give a behavior to the character

A mascot is not just about its appearance. Decide how she reacts: calm confidence, curiosity, theatrical mood or sporty energy. His poses and expressions must prolong this temperament. This continuity helps you imagine several scenes without reinventing its identity with each image.

Read the board view by view

Compare silhouette, profile, portrait and expressions. See if the clothes, proportions and colors remain compatible. Viral Trend characters have separate views to make reading easier; the mascot offer remains distinct from thematic photo packs.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

Before choosing a character, imagine three concrete situations in which you could use it. If his personality remains clear in each, you have a stronger narrative foundation than just a dramatic image. Then explore the cast of mascots.

Before choosing your images

  • A silhouette identifiable from afar.
  • An attitude that you can describe simply.
  • Continuity between the profile and the portraits.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
