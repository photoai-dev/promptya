Price of an AI photo shoot: compare the pack, the total and the uses

To compare an AI photo offer, look at what a pack includes and the total order amount. A lower unit price only makes sense if the number of collections and their use correspond to your project.

Read the four levels of Promptya

A pack of 100 photos is priced at €22.90. Two packs cost €39.80 in total, or €19.90 each. Three cost €50.70, or €16.90 each. Four cost €59.60, or €14.90 each. The discount depends on the packs combined in the same order.

Compare the complementarity of universes

A second theme can bring another decor, another material or an additional use. Define its role before adding it to the cart. Four relevant collections can form a campaign; several universes that are too close can especially increase the number of images to sort without enriching your project.

Distinguish offers that do not stack

The mascots have their own offer: €69.90 for 30 photos and a single buyer, depending on the conditions of sale. Musical covers also constitute a separate service, the price of which remains to be defined. These offers should not be compared as variations of the 100 photo theme pack.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

For a duo, assign a different function to each pack: a series of portraits in an urban universe and a more open series in a Mediterranean setting, for example. You are thus comparing complementary content, in addition to price.

Before choosing your images

  • The number of photos per pack.
  • The basket total, not just the unit price.
  • The concrete role of each chosen collection.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
