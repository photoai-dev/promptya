Get started right

Personalized AI photo: from portrait to real series

A personalized AI photo starts from an intention: to put a face in a setting, explore a style or build a collection. To obtain a useful series, we must first distinguish what must remain recognizable from what can change.

Choose what makes your image

Start with three elements: the subject, the atmosphere and the use. A portrait intended for a small vignette needs a readable face. An image to present a collection can give more space to the garment or decor. This hierarchy helps to choose the right universe before launching variations.

Understanding the role of the reference

A reference guides the model when a compatible mode is selected. It does not automatically lock all traits, accessories and details. A clear photo, without a strong filter, provides a basis that is easier to interpret. If resemblance matters, compare the portraits with each other before making your selection.

Moving from isolated images to collections

A collection gains strength when it maintains a common light, palette and intention. Vary the frame distance or pose rather than all features at once. You can then compose a set with an opening image, portraits and wider views.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

For a personal presentation, combine a close-up portrait with a wider shot in the same universe. Keep the outfit and palette close: the change in framing already creates enough variation.

Before choosing your images

  • Set publishing medium.
  • Choose a readable reference if necessary.
  • Check resemblance and details at actual size.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
