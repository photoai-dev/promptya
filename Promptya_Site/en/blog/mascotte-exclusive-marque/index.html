Choose an exclusive mascot for a brand or project

An exclusive mascot must correspond to the tone of your project and your uses. The choice is not limited to an original appearance: you must also understand what is proposed in the offer and what its conditions of sale provide.

Match personality to message

An imposing but calm character does not convey the same thing as a hurried or theatrical figure. Compare this temperament to the way your project speaks to the public. Imagine a first presentation and an everyday scene: the mascot must remain relevant in these two registers.

Evaluate the potential of different views

The portrait can serve as an announcement, while the silhouette helps to construct a scene or a banner. The expressions give a range of reactions. Observe the continuity of the character and the possibility of bringing him to life in several compositions, without depending on a single successful framing.

Understand the offer before purchasing

On Promptya, the offer displayed for a mascot is €69.90 for 30 photos, with a single buyer. The character is removed from the catalog after confirmation of the sale. The precise scope of the transfer depends on the conditions presented before payment: read them to check their suitability for your project.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

Prepare a sheet with the planned supports, the role of the character and the desired scenes. Then compare two mascots on this same basis. The decision becomes more precise than when you choose only the most spectacular board.

Before choosing your images

  • A tone compatible with your communication.
  • Useful views for your media.
  • Transfer conditions understood before purchase.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
