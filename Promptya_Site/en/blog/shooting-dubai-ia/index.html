Imaginary journeys

AI photo shoot in Dubai: choose between skyline and desert atmosphere

A universe inspired by Dubai can play on urban verticality, reflections and contrasts of materials. It becomes more precise when you choose a visual direction rather than a mixture of all the signs of luxury.

An urban story based on lines

A skyline, a glass facade or a street perspective can be enough to create a contemporary atmosphere. The character must maintain a readable size in these architectures. Choose a shot that connects his silhouette and the lines of the decor without making the face disappear in an overly vast landscape.

A more mineral palette for the desert

An atmosphere of sand and warm light tells a different story than a city night scene. Clothing materials can accompany this softness rather than compete with it. If you combine multiple collections, present these registers as separate sequences within the project.

Remember the signs useful to the project

A car, a shiny accessory and several monuments do not automatically make an image stronger. Choose a main element and examine the highlights, shadows and proportions. The creations suggest a universe; they do not constitute proof of a real presence in a place or establishment.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

For a contemporary series, start with a sober silhouette and graphic architecture. The automobile illustration serves here as a reference for material and attitude; consult the Dubai sheet to discover the visuals specific to the destination.

Before choosing your images

  • Choose an urban or mineral dominant.
  • Limit competing accessories.
  • Reread glass surfaces and reflections.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
