Get started right

Create a consistent AI photo series without repeating the same image

The consistency of a series does not consist of producing duplicates. It is based on a few stable benchmarks which make the images recognizable, even when the poses, distances and settings change.

Promptya Editorial · October 16, 2026 · 2 min read

Set three common benchmarks

Choose a palette, a clothing register and a family of light. These markers provide visual continuity. A set of cream tones in soft light remains identifiable with several framings, while a succession of contradictory colors and lighting can disperse the reading.

Vary one dimension at a time

You can alternate between a portrait, a silhouette and a context scene without changing the universe. This method allows you to understand what each variation brings. When all characteristics change at once, it becomes more difficult to know why an image deviates from the original plan.

Select for Rhythm

Build a sequence with breaths. A wide view can follow a tight portrait; a calm composition can balance a more dramatic image. Check that the character, colors and important details remain compatible. A short, well-organized series can serve your project better than a large set without selection.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

For a presentation carousel, start with a strong image, continue with a portrait then a detail and end with a context view. Each image provides different information while remaining in the same visual family.

Before choosing your images

  • Keep a recognizable palette.
  • Alternate shooting distances.
  • Eliminate variations that unintentionally change the subject.
