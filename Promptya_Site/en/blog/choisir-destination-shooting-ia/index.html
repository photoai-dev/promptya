Imaginary journeys

Which destination should you choose for an AI photo shoot?

The right decor is the one that serves your project. A well-known destination can catch the eye, but a series becomes more personal when its colors, lines and light match the way you want to be represented.

Promptya Editorial · October 16, 2026 · 2 min read

Starting from the palette and materials

Ask yourself if you are looking for light stone, urban colors or a Mediterranean setting. This first decision reduces the number of collections to compare. A cream outfit will easily interact with clear architecture; a more contrasting silhouette can stand out better in an already bright decor.

Decide on the place of the landscape

If the face is the main element, choose a decor that also works as a simple background. If the project focuses on the atmosphere of the place, plan more wide views. The format comes into play here: a vertical portrait and a horizontal banner do not give the same space to the environment.

Compare the files on concrete criteria

Look at the available overviews, the places mentioned and the uses described. Separate inspiration images from validated examples specific to the pack. You can put two destinations together when their roles complement each other, without assuming that more locations automatically improves your series.

AN EXAMPLE TO MOVE FORWARD

Putting this idea into practice

For a gentle series, compare Paris and the Cyclades according to their lines and their lights. For a more contrasting ensemble, consider a distinct urban direction. The filter by country and city helps to build this selection without browsing all the collections.

Before choosing your images

  • Define a common palette.
  • Choose the location of the decor facing the character.
  • Compare previews before destination name.

To prepare your series, also consult how the studio works and packages and their total amounts.
