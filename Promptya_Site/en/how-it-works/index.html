What is a Promptya AI Image Pack?

A pack is an order for visual creations around a theme: a destination, an atmosphere, a style or a creative universe. You choose the collection, then the studio prepares the scenes from our in-house library. You receive images, not the texts that are used to generate them.

How much do packs of 100 images cost?

A pack costs €22.90. For a group purchase, two packs cost €39.80 (€19.90 per pack), three packs €50.70 (€16.90 per pack), and four packs €59.60 (€14.90 per pack). Each pack contains up to 100 images. Discounts apply to the number of packs purchased together, not to separate orders.

Which reference photo to choose?

Choose a clear, well-lit photo with only one subject visible, without a strong filter or glasses obscuring the eyes. The accepted formats and limits are specified in the studio. Resemblance may vary depending on model.

Are creations delivered within two hours?

Two hours is a treatment goal, not a currently validated guarantee. The duration depends on the model, quantity, availability and possible returns. Order tracking allows you to view progress when the generation is connected.

How do I recover my images?

Completed creations are grouped in a ZIP archive that can be downloaded from your orders. Internal build instructions are not included. You will find your creations and their archive in your order space.

Do the previews show my future result exactly?

No. Validated previews show results achieved for a collection, but each new generation may vary. Until a pack has its own validated previews, its illustrations are marked as inspirations and are not presented as its results.
