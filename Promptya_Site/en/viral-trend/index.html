What makes a character memorable.

A strong mascot is recognized before even reading its name. A silhouette, a look, an expression or an unexpected detail gives the character its place in your world.

A personality before the effects.

Choose a character that matches the tone of your project: extravagant, mischievous, elegant or offbeat. The views of each profile allow you to compare your silhouette and expressions, beyond a single pose.

A common thread for your series.

The palette, clothing and distinctive features serve as benchmarks. The staging can then evolve to tell new situations while maintaining a recognizable identity.
