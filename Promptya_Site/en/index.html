PREPARE YOUR SERIES WELL

Choose a reference, a format and a rendering.

A readable photo

For a creation with reference, favor a visible face and regular light. Very dark glasses, filters and multiple subjects make reading difficult. The studio accepts one reference per order for the Kontext model.

A format adapted to the support

The square is suitable for a balanced composition. Portrait formats leave more room for a silhouette; the landscape gives more space to the decor. Choose according to your project rather than by multiplying formats in the same series.

Clear visual expectations

A model can interpret a setting, clothing or identity differently from another. Compare validated package previews and model capabilities. The consistency of a series also depends on the quality of the reference and the options used.

YOUR QUESTIONS

Packs, reference photos and AI generation.

The answers to understand the offer before choosing your collection.

What is a Promptya AI Image Pack?

A pack is an order for visual creations around a theme: a destination, an atmosphere, a style or a creative universe. You choose the collection, then the studio prepares the scenes from our in-house library. You receive images, not the texts that are used to generate them.

How much do packs of 100 images cost?

A pack costs €22.90. For a group purchase, two packs cost €39.80 (€19.90 per pack), three packs €50.70 (€16.90 per pack), and four packs €59.60 (€14.90 per pack). Each pack contains up to 100 images. Discounts apply to the number of packs purchased together, not to separate orders.

How do I create AI images with my photo?

Import a JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo into the studio, then choose a template compatible with the references. FLUX Kontext uses a reference image. A clear, well-lit photo without a strong filter makes it easier to read the face; final resemblance may vary.

What is the difference between FLUX Schnell, Kontext and a LoRA model?

FLUX Schnell produces images from a description. FLUX Kontext works from a visual reference. FLUX Dev LoRA allows the use of compatible customization weights. The references and LoRA are not interchangeable: the studio adapts the options to the chosen model.

Are creations delivered within two hours?

Two hours is a treatment goal, not a currently validated guarantee. The duration depends on the model, quantity, availability and possible returns. Order tracking allows you to view progress when the generation is connected.

How do I recover my images?

Completed creations are grouped in a ZIP archive that can be downloaded from your orders. Internal build instructions are not included. You will find your creations and their archive in your order space.

Do the previews show my future result exactly?

No. Validated previews show results achieved for a collection, but each new generation may vary. Until a pack has its own validated previews, its illustrations are marked as inspirations and are not presented as its results.

Can I use a photo of another person?

Use only references that you are authorized to use, with the consent of the people represented. The rules of the chosen model continue to apply. Importing a photo does not guarantee perfect preservation of identity on all images.
