Scène 01
A photorealistic scene of a woman (the model), standing next to a single, massive red LEGO brick that is taller than her. She is reaching out a hand to touch the texture of the plastic studs, looking up in awe at its immense size. The lighting is cinematic, highlighting the plastic sheen and surface scratches of the giant toy. (La Montagne de Plastique)
Scène 02
A photorealistic scene of a woman (the model), sitting carefully on the immense, soft paw of a giant, fluffy teddy bear. The bear's head and body disappear into the ceiling. She is smiling, using the plush fur as a cushion, emphasizing the softness and monumental size of the toy. (Câlin Géant)
